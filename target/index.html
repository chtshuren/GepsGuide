Government e-Procurement System Guide

快速了解政府電子採購網的重要功能 / A quick guide to key features of the platform

  • 1. 首頁 Homepage
  • 2. 廠商帳號註冊 Vendor Account Registration

1. 首頁 Homepage

中文這是政府電子採購網首頁, 點選廠商代碼下方的「廠商帳號申請」的連結。
ENThis is the homepage of the Government Electronic Procurement System, and you can click the "Vendor Account Application" link below the vendor code.

1-1. 註冊與登入 Registration & Login

中文註冊請點選廠商帳號申請, 登入請輸入帳號及密碼，延伸碼預設為0。
註冊連結為：https://web.pcc.gov.tw/raam/generalBusiRegistration/init
ENTo register, click the "Vendor Account Application" link. To log in, enter your account number and password. The extension code defaults to 0.
The registration link is: https://web.pcc.gov.tw/raam/generalBusiRegistration/init

2. 廠商帳號註冊 Vendor Account Registration

中文廠商帳號註冊，依照個人資料保護法規定，需要同意個人資料蒐集使用。
若為第一次使用政府電子採購網，類別可選擇個人。
ENVendor account registration requires consent to the collection and use of personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act.
If it is your first time using the Government e-Procurement System, you can select "Individual" as the account type.

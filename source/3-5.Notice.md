步驟1.領標說明
系統將會依照廠商帳號保留領標紀錄，後續登入將可以直接下載文件及取得領標憑據。
**請注意，若您使用不同的廠商帳號登入，將無法取得先前的領標紀錄及憑據。**
Step 1. Tender document instructions
The system retains tender document download records under the vendor account. When you log in again, you can download the documents and obtain the tender receipt directly.
**Please note that if you log in with a different vendor account, you will not be able to access the previous download records or tender receipt.**
